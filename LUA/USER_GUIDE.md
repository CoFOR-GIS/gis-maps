# Land Use Assumptions Viewer — User Guide

**City of Fair Oaks Ranch · Engineering Services**

The Land Use Assumptions (LUA) Viewer shows how every parcel in Fair Oaks Ranch is served by water and sewer utilities today, and how many connections the City expects at full build-out. The left side is an interactive map of parcels colored by their utility service. The right side is a live summary of connection counts that updates as you filter.

All figures are *subject to change* as parcels develop, plats are recorded, and utility records are updated.

---

## The layout

| Area | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Header filters** | Five dropdowns — County, Utilities, Parcel Status, Subdivision, Unit — that narrow both the map and the tables at the same time. |
| **Map (left)** | Every parcel, colored by its utility service. Click any parcel for its details, including acreage. Open the legend with the button in the top-right corner of the map. |
| **Connections table (right, top)** | Water and sewer connection totals by category for parcels served by Fair Oaks Ranch Utilities (FORU). |
| **Provider table (right, bottom)** | Counts of parcels served by other providers: SAWS, Enchanted Oaks, and private wells / no provider. |

## Using the filters

Each dropdown works the same way:

1. Click the dropdown to open it.
2. Type in the search box to find a value quickly.
3. Check one or more values. The map and every number on the right update immediately.
4. Check **Select all** to clear that filter back to "All."

Filters combine — choosing a Subdivision *and* a Parcel Status shows only parcels matching both. Every figure on the page responds to your filters, including Future (Planned) and Build Out.

## Reading the map

Click any parcel to see its subdivision, unit, utility provider, parcel status, **acreage**, and its current and master-planned connection counts. The legend (top-right button on the map) groups the colors into three sets:

**Fair Oaks Ranch Utilities** — parcels in FORU's service area, by what they have today:
- *Water Only / Water & Sewer* — a standard single water connection, with or without sewer.
- *Commercial (Water Only / Water & Sewer)* — the same, on commercial parcels.
- *Multiple Connections* — parcels with more than one water or sewer connection.
- *Non-Residential with Service* — greenspace, institutional, and other non-residential parcels with at least one connection.
- *Utilities Offered* — parcels within FORU's area where service is offered but no standard connection is recorded (includes government parcels and lots without active connections).
- *Not Serviced By A Utility* — parcels not served by any provider.
- *R.O.W.* — public and private rights-of-way.

**Other Utilities** — parcels served by the San Antonio Water System or Enchanted Oaks Utilities, and non-residential parcels with no service.

**Undeveloped Areas to be served by FORU** — parcels *Reserved* or planned as *Future Connections*.

## What the connection numbers mean

Each row counts **connections** (not parcels) for parcels where FORU is the provider. Where a small ⓘ appears beside a row name, click it for a plain-language definition.

- **Existing – Residential** — connections on improved (built) residential parcels.
- **Existing – Non-Residential** — connections on commercial, institutional, government, greenspace/drainage, right-of-way, and other non-residential parcels.
- **Permitted (New Homes)** — connections on parcels with an active building permit.
- **Vacant (No Permit)** — connections already assigned to vacant parcels without a permit.
- **Total** — the sum of the four rows above.
- **Future (Reserved)** — connections on parcels reserved for future development.
- **Future (Planned)*** — additional connections anticipated by the utility master plan beyond what exists today.
- **Future Total** — Future (Reserved) plus Future (Planned): all connections still to come at full build-out.
- **Build Out (%)** — connections in service today as a share of all connections expected at full build-out, shown with a progress bar.

The table is divided by rule lines into three groups: today's connections (through **Total**), future connections (through **Future Total**), and the build-out summary.

## The provider table

The lower table counts **parcels** (not connections) served by providers other than FORU. *Existing* = improved parcels, *Permitted* = parcels with an active permit, *Vacant (No Permit)* = undeveloped parcels with no permit, and *Total* sums the three.

## Refreshing

The viewer loads current data from the City's parcel layer when the page opens. Click **Refresh data** anytime — the line beneath it shows how many parcels are loaded and when.
